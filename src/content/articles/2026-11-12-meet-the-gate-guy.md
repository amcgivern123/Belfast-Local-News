For 25 years, Adrian has been installing, automating and repairing electric gates for homes and businesses. Many of his customers simply know him as The Gate Guy.

AutoGate NI is based in Belfast and works right across Northern Ireland, fitting new wrought iron, wooden, composite and aluminium gates as well as repairing the automation systems on existing ones.

## AutoGate NI - Electric Gate Installations

I installed my first electric gate system in July 2001. That's just over 25 years of installing, servicing and repairing electric driveway gates in Northern Ireland.   
That first installation was to automate an existing gate. The customer had wrought iron gates and was tired of opening and closing them by hand.

## The First Installation

### A practical reason to automate

In July 2001, I installed my first electric gate system for a customer who already had wrought iron gates. They wanted to stop opening and closing them manually, so I fitted automation to the gates they had rather than replacing them. That first job set a practical direction for my work: find out how the existing gates are used, then choose a system suited to the site and the customer’s routine.

## From Installation to Ongoing Care

### Servicing and Repairs

Installation is only one part of my work at AutoGate NI. I also service and repair electric driveway gates, giving you a point of contact when routine care or a fault arises. A service visit focuses on the gate’s operation and automation components, while a repair addresses a specific problem, such as a gate that no longer opens or closes as expected.

- Inspect hinges and moving joints for wear.
- Test safety edges and photocells.
- Check the control panel and wiring.
